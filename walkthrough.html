THE DEPTHS (Level 1-3)

Starting Area - Learn the Basics

Level 1: Awakening

You wake in a dark cell with only movement. Learning to dash is the point of this level.

ObjectiveLeave the cell, reach the first checkpoint
Key pickupEnergy orbs in the starting cell
TipsShort dashes to conserve; watch patrol loops

Level 2: First Falls

Vertical platforming. You fall deliberately while avoiding the spikes below, and the level ends with a wall-jump technique.

ObjectiveDescend the falling chambers
Key pickupWall Jump and a hidden energy cache
TipsPlatforms break under you; save energy for climbs

Level 3: The Guard Post

Your first real enemies. Guards patrol fixed routes and can be bypassed entirely.

ObjectiveReach the far door past the patrols
Key pickupEnergy from guards, optional
TipsGuards wind up before attacking; passing is safer

FORGOTTEN TUNNELS (Level 4-6)

Mid-Game - New Mechanics Introduced

Level 4: Flooded Passages

Water slows you and drains energy slowly. Dashing across quickly matters more than walking carefully.

ObjectiveCross the flooded passages to the vents
Key pickupCheckpoint and a tunnel orb
TipsWater drains 1% a second; air pockets refill

Level 5: The Mining Shafts

Crumbling platforms and narrow shafts. One wrong step means a long fall and heavy energy loss.

ObjectiveRide the minecart to the upper shaft
Key pickupExtended Dash and mineral deposits
TipsKeep a full bar for the minecart escape

Level 6: Echo Chambers

Sound-driven puzzles. Your dash echoes back and reveals hidden routes.

ObjectiveTrigger three echo reveals
Key pickupCheckpoint for the climb ahead
TipsLight footsteps on grates; a timed dash echoes

THE ABYSSAL CORE (Level 7-9)

Late-Game - High Challenge

Level 7: The Prison Ward

Undead prisoners wander the cell blocks. They are aggressive but predictable, and this level pays well for clean play.

ObjectiveClear the ward cells, reach the Warden
Key pickup50% energy behind the Warden
TipsUndead have no energy; the Warden is hazard-immune

Level 8: Gravity Wells

Gravity reverses without warning. Up becomes down, and low energy makes you drift helplessly.

ObjectiveCross three wells to the Core
Key pickup40% energy behind a false wall
TipsShifts come without warning; low energy makes you float

Level 9: The Heart

A massive slow creature that fills the room. Its attacks cover the floor.

ObjectiveDefeat The Heart without the floor
Key pickupGravity Anchor ability
TipsStay on the walls; attack after the third hit

SURFACE APPROACH (Level 10-12)

Final Stretch - Almost Free

Level 10: The Ascent

Almost entirely upward. Platform stability is the danger here and falls are expensive.

ObjectiveClimb to the light and halfway crystal
Key pickupMidway checkpoint
TipsFalls are costly; activate the checkpoint

Level 11: Light Tunnels

Sunlight cracks through the rock. The paths are narrower and beams hurt.

ObjectiveCross three beam sections using shadows
Key pickupFull restore in the shadow alcove
TipsBeams drain 5% a second; shadows are safe

Level 12: The Gate

The exit is guarded. Two patrols cross the hall and the gate needs no key.

ObjectiveSlip past both patrols, reach the gate
Key pickupFinal orb set for the true ending
TipsUse patrols against each other; the gate needs no key

ABILITY ACQUISITION ORDER

The intended progression, and why it works

Order Ability Level Unlocks
1 Wall Jump Level 2 Vertical shafts and the Level 2 cache
2 Extended Dash Level 5 Long gaps in the shafts and tunnels
3 Gravity Anchor Level 9 Precise platforming in Level 8 wells

Each ability opens a route backward, so the order never feels wasted. Stuck in Level 6 or 8? The ability you need is behind you.

BOSS STRATEGY CHEAT SHEET

Every major encounter in one place

Boss Level Opening Move Reward
Drowned Sentinel 4 Lantern flash, then a lunge. Dash sideways on the flash and punish the landing. Large orb and checkpoint
Echo Sentinel 6 Mirrors you. Stand still on a grate and it locks mid-lunge. Opens the echo door
The Warden 7 Shield bash, hazard-immune. Dash through, then hit the stagger. 50% energy
The Heart 9 Three-hit floor sweep. Stay on the walls, dash between swings. Gravity Anchor ability
Gate Captain 12 Charges the other guard. Pull it into a collision to stagger both. Access to the gate

FINAL ESCAPE

Freedom Awaits

Beyond the gate is a long stairway upward. No enemies, no traps. Just climb. The light grows with each step.

Final Tips:

  • • Energy regenerates here — climb at your pace
  • • The ending triggers when you reach the top
  • • Final energy level affects the ending cutscene
