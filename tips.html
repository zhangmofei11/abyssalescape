TIPS & TRICKS

Advanced strategies, hidden secrets, and expert techniques for escaping the Abyss.

ENERGY CONSERVATION

Master your most precious resource

Dash Only When Necessary

Running is free. Dashing costs energy. In safe areas, always walk. Save dashes for danger zones and precision platforming.

Short Dashes Over Long

Tapping dash gives 70% of the distance for 30% of the energy cost. Master the tap rhythm for maximum efficiency.

Abuse Invincibility Frames

Dashing grants 5 frames of invincibility. Use this to pass through hazards without taking damage. Timing is everything.

Energy Orb Priorities

Large orbs restore 20%, small orbs restore 5%. Always grab larges first. Small orbs in safe zones can wait.

COMBAT STRATEGIES

When fighting is unavoidable

The Stagger Method

First hit staggers most enemies, second hit kills. Two-tap dash attack combo is faster and safer than trying to melee.

Environment Kills

Lure enemies to spike traps or off ledges. This costs no energy and works on most regular enemies. The Warden is immune.

Escape Dash

If you're about to be hit, dash away. The i-frames will save you. A well-timed escape dash is better than tanking damage.

HIDDEN SECRETS

Areas most players miss

Secret Areas by Level

  • Level 2: Wall jump off the left side of the second falling chamber to find a hidden energy cache.
  • Level 4: Swim straight down at the end instead of going up — there's an underwater tunnel.
  • Level 6: Stand in the center and don't move for 10 seconds. The echo reveal opens a secret door.
  • Level 8: In the second gravity well, there's a false wall on the right that leads to 40% energy.
  • Level 11: In the third light beam section, there's a shadow alcove that gives full energy restore.

SPEEDRUN TECHNIQUES

For the fastest escapes

Corner Boost

Dashing into a corner at the right angle launches you faster than normal dash. The boost depends on angle — 45 degrees is optimal.

Extended Dash

Start dash at the peak of a jump. The dash extends your air time while maintaining speed. Useful for crossing large gaps efficiently.

Momentum Preservation

Don't dash perpendicular to your movement. Dash in the same direction to maintain momentum. Perpendicular dashes kill your speed.

Frame-Perfect Gravity Cancel

Enter a gravity well during a dash. If timed perfectly, you keep horizontal momentum. Very difficult but saves seconds in speedruns.

GENERAL ADVICE

Things that make the journey easier

Death is Not Punishment

Death costs energy, but respawning at checkpoints gives you another chance to optimize your route. Don't fear death — learn from it.

Checkpoints Are Sacred

Always activate checkpoints before attempting difficult sections. They're marked by glowing crystals. Standing near them restores 10% energy.

Study Enemy Patterns

Every enemy has a pattern. Watch for 2-3 cycles to learn it before engaging. Patterns don't change — they just get faster in harder areas.

Energy at 0% = Game Over

This is the only true fail state. Even touching enemies deals energy drain, not instant death. Keep even 1% energy and you can still recover.
