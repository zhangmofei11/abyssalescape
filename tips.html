TIPS & TRICKS

Advanced strategies, hidden secrets, and expert techniques for escaping the Abyss.

ENERGY CONSERVATION

Master your most precious resource

Dash Only When Necessary

Running is free. Dashing costs energy. Walk in safe areas and save dashes for danger zones and precision gaps.

Short Dashes Over Long

Tapping dash gives 70% of the distance for 30% of the cost. Master the tap rhythm for efficiency.

Abuse Invincibility Frames

Dashing grants 5 frames of invincibility. Use them to cross hazards without taking damage.

Energy Orb Priorities

Large orbs restore 20%, small orbs 5%. Grab larges first and leave small orbs in safe zones.

COMBAT STRATEGIES

When fighting is unavoidable

The Stagger Method

First hit staggers most enemies, second kills. The two-tap dash combo is faster and safer than melee.

Environment Kills

Lure enemies to spikes or off ledges. Free energy, and it works on most regular enemies. The Warden is immune.

Escape Dash

If a hit is incoming, dash away. The i-frames save you, and a timed escape beats tanking damage.

Where every secret room hides

  • Level 3 — Guard Locker: A cracked wall behind the third patrol opens during the pause.

NEW PLAYER MISTAKE LOG

The habits that cost the most energy

Mistake 1: Panic Dashing

Holding dash burns 10% per burst. Short taps at 3% cover the same jump.

Mistake 2: Ignoring the Bar

Below 50% your dash distance halves. Check the bar before a gap, not after you fall.

Mistake 3: Exploring Down First

Grabbing every orb in a side room leaves you depleted for the next boss. Clear the main path, farm later.

Mistake 4: Fighting Every Guard

Two hits cost more than the kill is worth. Pass first, farm on the way back.

Mistake 5: Skipping Checkpoints

A checkpoint restores 25% on activation and 10% after a death. Walking past one is the costliest mistake in the game.

Mistake 6: Replaying Sectors Badly

After a death you restart at the checkpoint with 0% energy. Take the route you know and collect on the return.

SPEEDRUN TECHNIQUES

For the fastest escapes

Corner Boost

Dashing into a corner at the right angle launches you faster than a normal dash. 45 degrees is optimal.

Extended Dash

Start dash at the peak of a jump to extend air time while keeping speed. Good for wide gaps.

Momentum Preservation

Dash in the same direction you are moving. Perpendicular dashes kill your speed.

Gravity Cancel

Enter a gravity well mid-dash and keep your horizontal momentum. Hard, but it saves real seconds.

SPEEDRUN ROUTE ORDERING

The cleanest progression line

  • 2. Take Wall Jump at the end of Level 2, then double back for the cache.
  • 3. Clear the Level 4 underwater tunnel, then take Level 5 for Extended Dash.
  • 5. Farm Level 7 cell blocks to full energy, then face the Warden.
  • 6. Enter Level 8 at full energy so gravity wells do not drain you, then push on.
  • 8. Climb Levels 10-12 without fighting, taking the Level 11 alcove on the way.

GENERAL ADVICE

Things that make the journey easier

Death is Not Punishment

Death costs energy, but respawning at checkpoints gives another chance to optimize. Don't fear death.

Checkpoints Are Sacred

Always activate checkpoints before hard sections. Standing near one restores 10% energy.

Study Enemy Patterns

Watch 2-3 cycles before engaging. Patterns never change, only speed up.

Energy at 0% = Game Over

This is the only true fail state. Enemies drain energy rather than killing outright, so even 1% keeps you alive.
