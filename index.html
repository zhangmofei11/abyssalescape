ABOUT THE GAME

You are a prisoner deep within the Abyss, an ancient underground prison that has held souls for millennia. The only way out is down — through forgotten tunnels, deadly traps, and the remnants of those who tried before you.

Every death drains your life energy. Run out completely, and you fade into the darkness forever. Conserve your energy, dash wisely, and find your way to the surface.
